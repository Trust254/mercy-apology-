Chapter 01 The woman I came to admire

It was more than beauty.

Dear Mercy, when I think about you, I remember a beautiful and kind woman with a humble heart, a playful spirit, and a hardworking nature. I admire your determination and the way you keep pushing forward. Those are the qualities that made you special to me.

The little moments we shared and the conversations we had when you were in Kenya meant something to me. They did not need to be grand to matter. You became the woman I cared for most, and I still value those memories.

I told my family about you because I was serious about getting to know you. They sometimes ask how you are doing, but I want you to know that their hopes are not a responsibility you must carry. Your feelings and your choice matter most.

Since we are both working in Qatar, life can be busy and communication can become difficult. I have missed the chance to sit down and speak with you properly. When I could not reach you, I felt uncertain, but I do not want to guess your reasons or speak for your heart. I would rather listen to your side.

Chapter 02 · Taking responsibility

Where I went wrong, I want to own it.

I know my communication was not good enough. I also understand that the situation involving your friend may have created doubt. She asked me to take some photos, and there was nothing romantic between us—but I should have communicated clearly and considered how it might look and feel to you. I am sorry for the confusion and for my part in letting trust become uncertain.

I cannot undo mistakes with a page on a website. I can only acknowledge them, learn, and show more maturity through my actions. I am sorry, Mercy.

Chapter 03 · What I hope you understand

Six things I should say plainly.

Not grand promises—just honest thoughts.

01.

I value who you are.

Your kindness, humility, humour, and hard work matter to me. My feelings are not just about your appearance.

02.

I am sorry for poor communication.

I should have made my intentions clearer and handled sensitive moments with more care.

03.

My gifts came without conditions.

I bought the watch and necklaces because I wanted to. They are not a debt, and you owe me nothing for them.

04.

I won't compare myself with other men.

I can only be honest about my own intentions and try to show care through consistent behaviour.

05.

I want to hear your truth.

If I hurt you or you see things differently, I am willing to listen without turning your feelings into an argument.

06.

Your choice is yours.

You do not have to forgive me quickly or return my feelings. I want any next step to be mutual and freely chosen.

“I don't want a gift to speak for me. I want honesty, respect, and my actions to show you the person I am.”
