Chapter one

The little moments that stayed with me

This isn't a demand for an answer. It's my way of saying what I should have communicated better.

Dear Mercy,

When I think about you, I remember a kind, beautiful, humble, playful, and hardworking woman. I admire your determination and the hunger you have to build a better life. Those qualities are what drew me to you—not just your beauty, but the person you are.

When we were communicating more easily while you were in Kenya, the small moments we shared meant a lot to me. They may have looked ordinary from the outside, but to me they were meaningful. You became the woman I cared for most, and those memories have stayed with me.

I also want you to understand a situation that may have caused doubt. Your friend once asked me to take some photos for her. There was nothing romantic or casual between us. But I understand that the way I handled things, and the way I communicated—or failed to communicate—may have made the situation look different. I should have been clearer, more considerate, and more careful with your feelings.

I told my family about you because I was serious about getting to know you. They became familiar with you through what I shared, and they have asked how you are doing. That is my choice and my responsibility; it should never become pressure on you or an expectation you must fulfil.

Since we are both working here in Qatar, life can be demanding, and distance can make communication harder. I have missed the chance to talk with you properly in person. When my calls have not connected, I have sometimes been left unsure how you feel. I don't want to guess your reasons or decide your feelings for you. I would rather listen to your side, if and when you feel comfortable sharing it.

I bought you a watch and necklaces some months ago because I wanted to give you something thoughtful. Those gifts were meant as gestures of care, not a price for your time, affection, or a meeting. You owe me nothing because of them. What matters most to me is honesty, mutual respect, and the chance to communicate openly.

I am not writing this to compare myself with other men or to make promises just to impress you. I can only speak for myself: I want to be truthful with you, show care through my actions, and improve the communication that I know I neglected.

With respect and a sincere heart,

Someone who cares about you, Mercy.

Chapter two

I'm sorry for the ways I let you down.

Mercy, I take responsibility for my part. My communication was not good enough, and my behaviour around a situation involving your friend may have made you uncomfortable or caused you to question my intentions. Even if I did not mean to hurt you, I recognise that my actions may still have hurt you.

I'm sorry for not explaining things clearly, for not giving reassurance when it mattered, and for allowing silence and misunderstanding to grow between us. I should have handled things with more maturity. I can't change the past by writing a beautiful page, but I can learn from it and behave better.

I care about you, but I also respect your freedom to decide what you want. You do not have to forgive me quickly, meet me, or return my feelings because I have apologised. If your feelings have changed, I would rather hear the truth kindly than keep guessing.
